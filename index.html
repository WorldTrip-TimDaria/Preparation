                          Every spot we found

                          Renting the van

                          Prices as published in the guides below. Weekday, weekend and peak rates differ, so a weekday pick-up and return is cheaper. A kei camper avoids the big vans' problems: nearly 3 m high, no multi-storey car parks, scraping on steep side roads, and pushed around by strong wind.

                          Renting the Hijet from Evertrail

                          • Price isn't published: ask for a quote for 14 nights (15 February to 1 March 2027).
                          • Insurance is fully comprehensive; if we cause a crash we pay up to ¥300,000, or nothing with the optional waiver chosen at booking.
                          • Getting there: bus 111 or 117 from the airport, about an hour, ¥1,330 each; or Evertrail's shuttle at ¥10,000 for two, one way (10:30 to 16:00). Earliest pick-up 9:30.
                          • Cancellation: full refund 60 days or more before, then 80% (30–59 days), 60% (7–29 days) and less after.
                          • Drivers must be 21 or over. Evertrail can book campsites for us and suggest wild spots; off-road driving and unapproved beaches are banned.

                          Before we go

                          • Roadside stations (michi-no-eki) are for breaks, shopping and information, not overnight stays: sleep at campsites or RV parks.
                          • Arrive before dark: spots are hard to find after sunset, especially the free ones.
                          • Book the van at the same time as the flights, and use the rental company's online price calculator. Expect about an hour of handover before you leave.
                          • Driving licence: an International Driving Permit issued in France is not valid in Japan. We each need an official Japanese translation of our French licence (from JAF or the French embassy or consulate in Japan), carried with the licence and passport. Allow about two weeks; one agency quotes €70 per person.
                          • Fly between Okinawa and Kyushu: the ferry takes about 25 hours and costs more.
                          • Car parks at tourist sites can have height or length limits. Don't run the engine or air conditioning while sleeping, and don't leave windows open overnight.
                          • At pick-up, check how the fuel cap opens and the refuelling rules; fill the water tank before relying on an on-board shower.
