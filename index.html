                        The loop

                        Okinawa City as a base for the south, then the wild north-east coast and Yanbaru forest, a long stop on the Motobu peninsula for Kouri and the aquarium area, and back to Okinawa City to return the van.

                          Every spot we found

                          Renting the van

                          Prices as published in the guides below. Weekday, weekend and peak rates differ, so a weekday pick-up and return is cheaper. A kei camper avoids the big vans' problems: nearly 3 m high, no multi-storey car parks, scraping on steep side roads, and pushed around by strong wind.

                          Renting the Hijet from Evertrail

                          • Price isn't published: ask for a quote for 14 nights (15 February to 1 March 2027).
                          • Insurance is fully comprehensive; if we cause a crash we pay up to ¥300,000, or nothing with the optional waiver chosen at booking.
                          • Getting there: bus 111 or 117 from the airport, about an hour, ¥1,330 each; or Evertrail's shuttle at ¥10,000 for two, one way (10:30 to 16:00). Earliest pick-up 9:30.
                          • Cancellation: full refund 60 days or more before, then 80% (30–59 days), 60% (7–29 days) and less after.
                          • Drivers must be 21 or over. Evertrail can book campsites for us and suggest wild spots; off-road driving and unapproved beaches are banned.

                          Before we go

                          • Roadside stations (michi-no-eki) are for breaks, shopping and information, not overnight stays: sleep at campsites or RV parks.
                          • Arrive before dark: spots are hard to find after sunset, especially the free ones.
                          • Book the van at the same time as the flights, and use the rental company's online price calculator. Expect about an hour of handover before you leave.
                          • Driving licence: an International Driving Permit issued in France is not valid in Japan. We each need an official Japanese translation of our French licence (from JAF or the French embassy or consulate in Japan), carried with the licence and passport. Allow about two weeks; one agency quotes €70 per person.
                          • Fly between Okinawa and Kyushu: the ferry takes about 25 hours and costs more.
                          • Car parks at tourist sites can have height or length limits. Don't run the engine or air conditioning while sleeping, and don't leave windows open overnight.
                          • At pick-up, check how the fuel cap opens and the refuelling rules; fill the water tank before relying on an on-board shower.

                              Renting the van

                              A 4-berth is 7–7.5 m long and nearly 3 m high, with two double beds: fine for two couples. Prices as published; quote the exact dates before booking.

                              Before we go

                              • Freedom camping: since 7 June 2026 only vehicles with a green self-containment warrant (a fixed toilet; portable ones no longer count) can stay at council spots reserved for self-contained vehicles. Ask the rental company to confirm the van's warrant in writing. Holiday parks and DOC campsites take any vehicle.
                              • Winter roads: the Desert Road, Haast Pass, Crown Range, Lindis Pass and Milford Road can have snow and ice. On the Milford Road chains must be carried when the signs say so (June to November), with a NZ$750 fine. Wilderness rents chains for NZ$70, or includes them in Full Cover. Check the road status every morning.
                              • The Tongariro Alpine Crossing in winter is snow, ice and avalanche terrain, needing crampons and an ice axe: go with a guide or skip it. Shuttles don't run in winter.
                              • Ferry: book the Interislander or Bluebridge in advance. A van up to 8 m costs about NZ$505–545 at one reseller, plus passengers; booking through the rental company can be cheaper. Crossings are cancelled in strong wind, so keep a spare day.
                              • Diesel jumped in 2026 (up to about NZ$3.50 a litre in April) and was NZ$2.45–2.68 by July–September; the budget uses NZ$2.60. Without Full Cover, road user charges for diesel (about NZ$8.40 per 100 km) are billed after the hire.
                              • Driving: on the left, with many one-lane bridges and slow winding roads; plan on about 70 km/h on average. Sunset is around 6 pm: arrive at the campsite before dark.
                              • Licence: a French licence works for up to 12 months with an International Driving Permit or an approved English translation.

                              Working holiday jobs around Auckland

                              With a working holiday visa (PVT) we can take any temporary job for up to 12 months. This page has the visa steps, the first-week paperwork, the jobs that exist around Auckland in August (late winter) and what the minimum wage pays after tax.

                              €—

                              At the adult minimum wage, NZ$23.95/h since 1 April 2026 (it usually rises each April), plus 8% holiday pay. Tax is as for a main job (code M) plus the ACC levy, so the figure is rough. Three weeks of work leaves the first days of August for paperwork.

                              The visa (PVT)

                              France–New Zealand working holiday scheme, from Immigration New Zealand's page.

                              First week in Auckland

                              • Get a New Zealand SIM: employers, banks and job apps all ask for a local number.
                              • Open a bank account (ANZ, ASB, BNZ, Kiwibank or Westpac).
                              • Apply online for an IRD (tax) number with our passport and visa application number (on the approval letter, not the visa number). Guides disagree on whether a New Zealand bank account is needed first; if it is, apply with the account, otherwise add it later.
                              • Give each employer a tax code form (IR330): M for the main job; a second job uses SB, S or SH depending on income.
                              • Casual and short jobs usually add 8% holiday pay to each payslip instead of paid leave.
                              • Before leaving the country, file a tax return: a few months of work is taxed as if it lasted a year, so part of the tax usually comes back.

                              Jobs we could do in August

                              August is late winter: hospitality is quieter, kiwifruit pruning is ending and the ski season is in full swing in the south. Pay figures come from job listings seen in 2025–2026.
